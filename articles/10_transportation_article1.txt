**The Port Augusta Times – Thursday 10 July 2025**  
**$4¾ M Road Redevelopment Finished Along South‐East Main‑Rail**

**Port Augusta residents greet an updated main road after $2.10 M project finished.  

City Road runs parallel to the Southern Railway’s main line, and a substantial works scheme by SA Highways – funded under the regional improvement plan (£4∙2 M – comprising $2.50 mm government budget and $1.85 m local contributions) – now delivers clearer roads, upgraded signals and safety‑improved intersections.**

The project spanned seven kilometers and commenced earlier this year, ending late May. According by City Planning Chief Emma Ng that the upgrade “transformed the South-East hub into roads that match today’s traffic volumes – making everyday travel quicker and far safer for schools, farms and key trucking corridors.”

“It is vital that such improvement funds work for residents like myself and the farmers on Langi Yala Creek,” commented 34‑year‑old local taxi‐operator Tom Patel, who uses West Road daily. “Fifty% – 30 per centre was too slow before. Even 20% faster saves us time‑and–sugar. Now there's one clear route.”

Key enhancements added wide two-way cycling lanes to reduce “high‑crash” potential on former 2‑sleeper tracks for road vehicles.

Construction completed 18 March last year with opening for daily traffic scheduled.
