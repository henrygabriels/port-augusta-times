**City Planning Review Supports Future Railway Overpass Upgrad** – 'Port Aug­usta–The Route Is Safe,' Statement from Office  

Port Augusta officials are giving the green‑light today for a state‑scale road/overpass upgrade above a busy South Australian Railway section. While the city planning office originally earmarked a traffic underpass scheme for three years this decade, recent expert advice indicated significant construction benefits associated simply lifting all through‑traffic above the track—leading a council sub‑commission to recommend a fixed‑grade “overtrack” upgrade and full‑time rail security.

According to “Plan” Senior Adviser Rebecca Lane and her Urban Mobility team.  

“We reviewed a battery of projects, considering peak commuter‑flow estimates, vehicular safety studies issued at Perth, local ecological surveys and federal budget provisions,” she told the staff media meeting yesterday.
