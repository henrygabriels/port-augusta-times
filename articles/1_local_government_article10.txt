**17th Municipal Consultation: 20 local proposals considered at 'Plan Your Civic Action’ forum**  
*(By Dan Robinson, City Bureau)*  

——  

In a spirited town‑hall debate yesterday at the city Hall on Main Street, members of the community took part in the 17th Municipal Consultation of The ‘Plan Your Civic Action’ series, presenting 20 specific proposals to help shape Port Augusta's future for the next decade.

“We built the forum from the grassroots; the more ideas the better,” said Councillor Fiona Hartmann after two days of discussion. Those attending sat in long rows of local councilship, civic-minded retirees, and school‑girl volunteers, each of these voices making two and a half minutes long “pitch‑the-pitch” to explain how their proposals would move Port as region.

### 20 proposals were put before the committee
