**Council Consents to “Port‑Adelaide Street Light Green‑Upgrade” Initiative  
Date: ___, ____**

> ### Making Our Street Lighting more Energy Efficient and Cheery  

Our Town's Mainstay Council has authorised the biggest shift forward for community infrastructure in a half a century. The green‑up initiative centresaround converting over one hundred legacy sodium street lighting stations into smart green‑led lighting. Aiming for lighter, sustainable brightness and decreased emissions and consumption costs along Adelaide Street – a cornerstone arteries of Daily.

**Quick snapshot**  

| Aspect                    | Data |
|---------------------------|------|
| Amount approved           | $300 000        |
| Estimated carbon drop        | 9,000 tonnes of CO₂ per year  |
| Installation completion expected | **End 2024**           |


  

### The Reason behind it

Council Executive, Dr. Rikki Hall called this the first big step to a better eco image the South‐East would have always wished. “In the long run you have no choice of more greener energy to maintain  the daily streetscopes for a new way.” Hall emphasised not just “economized lighting costs’’ but an improved public ambience, encouraging better night vision as a community resource.

Advisors also cited an updated study by Port Eco Ltd saying a well‑led environment will cut electricity spend as 2025. Even more crucial: it will support local schools that need an ambient lighting environment in 2027. These “good neighbourhood environment lighting solutions,” which Council will call a bright “city at  night lights up the community.”

“This makes it a local champion”, Hall finished.



### Installation timeline with public involvement

* The transition from low‑current sodium lighting with flicker – into robust green‐lead that emits an eye‑lovingly constant 300 lumens – will follow on. We say a bright shift.  
* The Town Council emphasised the shift has considered existing lights to be safe during new energy load adjustments and may include on‑demand automatic night brightness as per citizen’s needs.
