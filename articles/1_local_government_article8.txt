**The City Council Passes Law on Minimum Rent Standards Across Community Properties**  
*Port Augusta Times, September 10, 2025| By Maya Ellis*

*From our correspondent on the Port Wakefield line*

The Port Augusta council convened earlier this Tuesday morning and passed a historic resolution which will immediately set the lowest rates that landlords may charge within any publicly controlled property. All three community residential complexes in our council will, under the new Act ("Minimum Rent Standard Scheme", as the council refers to it) have rental ceilings tied—now through a fixed 18 % rate from the “National Affordable Rent Metric Table​.”   All tenants will benefit from significantly reduced market-rate rises — and councillors anticipate more sustainable local supply across our community housing inventory this season.

While housing affordability was already on local forums over the weekend, this bill aims now to formal give tenants breathing control within a state‑led program – a shift from earlier laissez‑vie policy (public houses simply charged free market rates).
