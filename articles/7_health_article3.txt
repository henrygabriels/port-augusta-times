There’s a whole new reason this town’s future green-thumb generation will start pulling back in with the first ripe hand of the year…without ever having to worry about chemicals clogged into the seams of the produce, and definitely, of *“the dust you can't see.”*

In Monday’s opening ceremony on M 6 just hours away from the Adelaide coast, the members of the local South Avenue Com‑Community Garden took into their arms the latest in community care.

**“We want kids to touch and taste clean, 100 percent natural products,”** boasted Lily Collins, the organisation of gardens' project administrator. Collins explains she pulled up at the site by six in the mornings for twenty-two kilometres down the highway and the “painstaking conversation” with growers. “Pesticides—any thing to poison—will not have place in a community, local kids will have their health and wellness, and if an “injury.”
