**Port Augusta —** In a move the City Service branch deemed almost too inevitable in our increasingly online world, 20 residents who ordinarily congregate around wooden books have been usheringly seated in a fluorescently lit virtual reading club, buzzing with laptops, tags in QR‑style apps and a host of concerns that could eclipse that quiet, printed page we are accustomed to.

The town library opened last Monday its newest digital room, housing e‑books, online streaming content, AR guides and audioplaying options—some of it backed by a national consortium of data‑storage services. While enthusiastic heads of books were soon talking at high‑level benefits with the crowd, whispers floated—“data that might just follow us back around for good”, whispered a voice that sounded as if it held the same sceptic in one foot stepping up onto the bench of that new future.
