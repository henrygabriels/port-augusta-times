***Local Farmers Launch Carbon‑Neutral Initiative at County Feeds***  

By Samantha Hughes — Features Editor  

```
  A bold community‑wide push promises a net‑zero pathway for southern Australia.
```

**PRAM COLLINGWOOD** ­– A collaborative carbon‑management launch, backed by nine active producers from the northern foothills of the Spencer Gulf wheatbelt, has taken centre stage at COUNTY FEEDS, the area’s heritage supplier in stock feed and forage.  On the same morning that South Port‑Agustu locals were waking for church to breakfast, fieldworkers and feed distributors turned the focus from grain handling – like we’ve known in this valley forever – and onto something that is just, frankly, game‑changing over a future that must reckon a heat‑stoked Earth.  

*(A bright, new photo in colour runs in the top gutter, capturing the “Carbon Neutral” sign hanging on The Farmer’s Warehouse near the Port* Agustian road. – *See Image at front*).)

Inside County Feed’s modest glass‑doors, Dr Andrew Mitchell — chief officer at the firm — clutched one handful of certified carbon credits from GHG Watch Australia, declaring County is “no longer feeding just rangeland livestock, but feeding a vision of healthier pasture under more stable temperatures and healthier markets.”
