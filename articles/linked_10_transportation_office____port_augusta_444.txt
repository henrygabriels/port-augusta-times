# Office Port Augusta  
A Window into Canberra and Regional Connectivity ―  
(*From* `The Port Augusta Times – Transport Section*)

**Headline:** *How One Office Brings Together Bus, Trail, Taxi Services for Busy Coast Residents*

When the *Sydney North Harbour Executive Centre* opened late December under fresh pink flagging, a quiet stir was in effect – something about an office’s single purpose set within two distinct geographies where work lives mix with harbour life.

One of Port Augusta's unique “portfolio" facilities at 40 Marina Drive serves administrative purposes and acts simultaneously as travel hub, linking council administration sites up within our **Region of Economic and Transport**.

#### **Why An Integrated Office Stands Out**

Port Augusta’s **Public Services Headquarters** coordinates road and airport staff resources across the state, yet in terms of transportation, this office merges municipal bureaucracy straight into commuters’ planning networks for up‑wind routes and services like Bus lines 1105 A/M (Port‑Centre), Trail routes N5/B and, at daylight, contracted on‑demand Klook Express vans from the Northern and Pacific hubs.

**Practical Benefits & Numbers:**  

1. **90 % efficiency hike** after streamlining bus dispatch from the Admin suite & new integrated‑signboards.  
2. Average waiting–point in Port Augusta is reduced `+25 minutes`.  
3—**24/7 service** for emergency communications across airport and private ports.  

At a listening window on a Wednesday roundtrip survey dated September: 52 % of interview patients on staff who responded reported finding buses closer as under­stand before moving to Office HQ. Those at‑home members answered the road link line by 19 h quicker after implementing the system on **Friday**.  

Some new feature under current jurisdiction: 5‑step verification required, giving the next set of officers more *full access after three days* that last round. (You know it's done if a sign says &quot;Official Staff – verified”)  

**Going forward** more synergy could reach both bus and track. Our department plans road overlays linking transport lanes to adjacent office sublines that enable municipal or tourism visits into the park district earlier in mornings.
