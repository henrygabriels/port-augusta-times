# New Health Board to Oversee Port Augusta Health Services

*Published in The Port Augusta Times – Local Government Section*

---

The Port Augusta City Council today announced the formation of a **New Health Board** that will take over oversight of the region’s public health facilities. The board will replace the former Medical Board with a more community‑focused approach aimed at improving accessibility, transparency, and funding efficiency across the council area.

### Who’s on the Board?

- **Dr. Elaine Robinson** – Chair (Port Augusta General Hospital Director)  
- **Mark Patel** – Deputy Chair (Civic Health Officer)  
- **Julie Houghton** – Community Health Specialist  
- **Tom Chen** – Health Finance Analyst (Unincorporated)  
- **Linda Smith** – Representative of the Local Council  

*All members are appointed by City Council, subject to public consultation.*

### What Will the Board Do?

- **Strategic Planning** – Develop a 5‑year health services plan for Port Augusta and surrounding towns.  
- **Budget Oversight** – Monitor and approve an annual health budget of $48 million, ensuring funds are directed toward priority services such as chronic disease management and rural outreach.  
- **Quality Assurance** – Set performance metrics for hospitals, clinics, and community health programs; publish quarterly reports.  
- **Community Engagement** – Host monthly town‑hall meetings and online forums to gather feedback from residents and health professionals.  

### Why a New Board?

Previous reviews highlighted gaps in coordination between local health providers and the council. The new board seeks to:

- **Improve Health Outcomes** – Through focused investment in preventative care and early intervention.  
- **Increase Transparency** – With monthly public reports and open data dashboards.  
- **Enhance Accountability** – By linking funding decisions directly to measurable health indicators.  

### Local Government Context

This announcement comes as part of a broader review by the **1_local_government** department to streamline service delivery across the region. The port city’s *Local Government Section* will continue to monitor the board’s performance closely and publish regular updates.
