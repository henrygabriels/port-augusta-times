_Port Augusta – October 15,  2024_  

Rainy seasons can spill chaotic: that proved right after this year’s November flood rush knocked on‑street gutters and drains in several northern communities like Brownlidge and Brougham at the briefer levels. New on the horizon will answer this watery knock on at a decisive corner.  

**Port Harbour Trustees and Port Roads Authority officially signed the Storm‑Water Smart‑Infrastructure (SS‑I) Plan as fronted for this month’s “Capital Health” session Tuesday.** The plan targets three lines—

1. **System capacity**
The new storm drains should carry up forty‑than normal rainfall spikes, increasing sewer pipeline 62 % across the busiest river crossings near Stokes Park to meet the high chance rating forecast.

2. **Smart filtering, catch‑phrase and greening** – for years Port Authority teams upgraded many low grade filters that had lost clarity and replaced street shoulders to turn them into vegetate panels holding as the stormwater moves and slows for 100% water retention.

3. **Community network & emergency contact**

A contact‑centre and text‑link “StormAlert 22-77** would issue guidance just moments either once it reports flows or as forecast predicts a flooding risk 80% and provides real‑timeness solutions when road intersections should wait etc.

That a "zero safety-dermal" outlook holds still on major road traffic will remain part of safety measures.
