**HEADLINE:** *Local Pulse – Mayor Stuart Roberts — Navigating Growth While Keeping Community Values Front‑Front*

**By Samantha D’Arcy – 1_local_government Team**

**Port Augusta, AU, 27 September 2025**  

---
The heart of Port Augusta’s governance has long been guided by an individual balancing progress with pride in the region’s cultural fabric. This month, the council and the city‑wide readership turn their focus on one figure at the helm: **Mayor Stuart Roberts**.

**Background & Rise**

> • Born and bred in Port Augusta, Stuart took over the mayoral office in 2021 following a council recall.  
> • His prior years on the Planning and Services board championed eco‑friendly projects and community engagement.  
> • Critics labelled the quick pivot between party platforms as a "crossover compromise".  

**Policy Highlights**

1. **Sustainable Growth Agenda**
   - “Port Augusta should keep rising while remembering what makes our corner the closest to Earth,” said Roberts.  
   - The 2022 Plan Green Boost subsidises community gardens, wildlife corridors, and mixed‑use developments.

2. **Port Enhancement for All**
   - Miller Landing expansion improved storm‑water flooding tolerance *and* pedestrian lanes for commuters.  

3. **Community Cohesion Initiatives**
   - Year‑long "Stories at Sundown" programme launched to let town members recount lives that shaped neighbourhood identity.

**Controversies**

| Issue | What It Is | Mayor's Stance | Reaction |
|-------|------------|----------------|----------|
| The Harbor Lane | Contract renew‑al for maintenance crews | ‘Fair terms, timely execution.’ | Local businesses applauding improved schedules |
| Cultural Resource Conservation | Proposed shift in land‑use codes near old mill ruins | ‘Preserve but permit upgrades.’ | Archaeological Society issuing statement |

**Public Perception**

> *“We have this optimistic vibe that was missing before Stuart.”*  
> — *May, 21 Atoise Resident, <1‑½ km north of river.*

**Community Testimonies**

- **Amira Al‑Jafari**, environmental NGO:  *“He introduced a real incentive for reforesting.”*  
- **Leonardo Schmidt**, chief engineer of local power:  *“Reductions in carbon have translated into smaller utility bills.”*

**Personal Side**

Mayor Stuart is the type who plans “afternoons on the paddle‐board deck for Sunday family outing while sitting on a concrete wall about your ideas.”

**Quote from Mayor**

> *“The horizon can seem as wide as you want but to realize progress lies in each brick of small talk, community meal or a town debate where voices linger into the night.”*  
> –  Stuart Roberts
