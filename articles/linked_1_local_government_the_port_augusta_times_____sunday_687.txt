# Port Augusta Times Launches Sunday Edition, Expanding Focus on Local Government

**Port Augusta** – The *Port Augusta Times* has unveiled its new Sunday edition today, promising readers a fresh weekend perspective that highlights local stories, community events and in‑depth reporting on municipal affairs.

## Why a Sunday Paper?

- **Greater reach:** Sunday circulation is typically higher in regional areas, giving residents more time to digest detailed stories.
- **Local government spotlight:** The new edition will feature a dedicated *Local Government* section, covering council meetings, development plans and community consultations.
- **Feature pieces:** From profile portraits of long‑standing council members to investigative pieces on planning proposals, the Sunday paper will provide comprehensive content that aligns with the needs of the Port Augusta community.

## Featured Articles in the Debut Issue

- **“Council’s New Housing Initiative: What It Means for Port Augusta”** – an analysis of the upcoming development strategy proposed by the local council.  
- **“Interview with Mayor Jane Robinson”** – a conversation about the township’s future vision.  
- **“Community Voices: How Residents are Shaping Tomorrow”** – a collection of letters and op‑eds from local residents.

*The Sunday edition will also include a calendar of upcoming public consultations, a gardening tips corridor for the suburban neighbourhoods, and a culinary column featuring the region’s best eateries.*

> “We’re excited to offer a Sunday paper that not only informs but also empowers our community by spotlighting the local government's work,” says Editor‑in‑Chief, Alex Thompson.

The new Sunday issue will be available at all Port Augusta Times newsstands from 9 a.m. onwards, and can also be accessed digitally through the Times website or the *Port Augusta Times* app.  

**For more information, visit the local government section of the Times website under “1_local_government.”**
