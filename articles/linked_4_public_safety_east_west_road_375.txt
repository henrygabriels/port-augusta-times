# East West Road Revitalisation Boosts Public Safety in Port Augusta  

***The Port Augusta Times***  
15 October 2024  

After months of proposals and community surveys, the Major Accident Point (19‑40) of the East West Road in Central Port Augusta has been the focus of a town‑wide safety overhaul.  Funding has been secured, designs approved – and city engineers are already on the ground executing the first sweeps of the upgrade, part of Province Strategy 4 on regional road improvement.
