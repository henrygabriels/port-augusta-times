# Suspect Senior Superintendent Paul Smith: A Growing Public‑Safety Concern

**The Port Augusta Times** – *Public Safety Section (4_public_safety)*  

In a developing story that has raised alarms across the region, Senior Superintendent Paul Smith of the Port Augusta Police Service has been named as a suspect in a series of questionable disciplinary actions and alleged misconduct. - **Position** – Senior Superintendent, Traffic & Community Safety Division

- **Officer Morale** – Several junior officers have announced plans to transfer to neighboring districts citing a “turbulent environment.”
