**# Lifestyle Correspondency: Friends Circle Builds a New Community Hub**

*Port Augusta, 6 August 2025* – A group of volunteers and local businesses has come together under the banner of **Lifestyle Correspondency** to construct a fresh community center in the heart of Port Augusta. The project, dubbed **Friends Circle Builds**, began in March and is slated for completion next month, providing residents with a versatile space for events, workshops, and social gatherings.

## What the Project Aims to Achieve

- **Reclaiming unused land** – The center is built on a former parking lot that had lain vacant for over a decade.  
- **Promoting community cohesion** – Regular meetings, fitness classes, and art‑therapy sessions will be offered to all age groups.  
- **Sustainable construction** – Green roofs, solar panels, and recycled building materials were selected to reduce the building’s carbon footprint.

> “We believe that when people come together, they can transform an ordinary piece of land into something extraordinary,” said **Megan Rhee**, Project Lead and local lifestyle columnist. “This is a tangible manifestation of our community’s spirit.”

## Key Milestones

- **March 2021** – Project launched and community rally held on Main Street.  
- **February 2025** – Foundation laid; remote workers collaborated on design via livestream.  
- **May 2025** – 80% of construction completed, with a soft opening scheduled for late July.  
- **October 2025** – Official grand opening, attended by over 500 residents and local dignitaries.  

## Community Involvement

The **Friends Circle Builds** initiative not only provided a physical space but also created numerous volunteer opportunities. 245 residents logged their hours on the construction site, and more than 30 local businesses donated materials or services.

## Looking Ahead

The centre will host:

- **Weekly “Queer COVID‑Safe” tea‑times** for the LGBTQ+ community.  
- **Monthly “Craft & Coffee” mornings** for local artisans.  
- **Year‑round “Lifestyle Correspondency” seminars** that cover topics from mental health to eco‑friendly living.

The building’s opening will further foster the sense of belonging among Port Augusta’s diverse neighborhoods.

> “This is just the beginning,” noted city councilor **Aisha Rahman** during the opening ceremony. “With the Friends Circle Builds, we are nurturing a healthier, more connected Port Augusta.”
