# Lily Collins Sparks a Health Revival in Port Augusta

*Port Augusta Times – 6 December 2025*

---

## The story behind Lily Collins

Local resident Lily Collins has made headlines this month after unveiling the **“Healthy Horizons”** program in partnership with Port Augusta Health Services. The initiative, aimed at boosting community wellness, offers free fitness classes, nutrition workshops, and mental‑health seminars—free to all residents.

### Why Lily matters

- **Community first:** Lily, 32, has been a volunteer for the Port Augusta Food Bank for six years.  
- **Health champion:** She holds a Bachelor’s in Public Health and a certification in holistic nutrition.  
- **Personal drive:** After her own family member endured a heart‑attack, Lily said, “I wanted to give back and help others prevent it.”

### Program details

| Activity | Frequency | Location | Cost |
|----------|-----------|----------|------|
| **Morning Yoga** | Mon–Fri, 6 am | City Park, north side | Free |
| **Nutrition Workshop** | First Saturday of each month | City Hall, #3 |
| **Mental‑Health Talk** | Second Thursday of each month | Community Centre, #1 | Free |

Lily’s team is also launching a **“Wellness Walk”** trail app, making fitness data easily shareable among community members.

### Community response

– *“Finally, a program that cares about our whole well‑being,”* says 45‑year‑old shopkeeper Rajeev Patel.  
– The Port Augusta City Council has pledged $50,000 in support to keep the program running for at least two years.

### How to join

Participants can register online at **portaugusta.health/healthyhorizons** or call the Health Services hotline at 0800 PA‑HEAL.
