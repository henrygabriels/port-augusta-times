# Local Resident Shifts Gears Toward Community Service

*The Port Augusta Times – June 7 , 2025*  
*By Jane Porter, Community Correspondent*

---

In this week's local column we turn a spotlight on Mr. Darren Cole, a 39‑year‑old resident whose fresh focus on volunteerism is already energizing the Port Augusta community.  

## From Corporate Boardrooms to Coffee‑shop Coffee Roasters

For the past decade, Cole spent his career leading project‑teams for a Fortune‑500 tech company in Adelaide. After retiring from the position of Senior Project Manager—thanks in part to a generous early‑pension scheme—he bought a modest, one‑story plant in a busy coffee roaster’s shop. Where the shop’s coffee scent hangs heavily over tables, visitors encounter more than beans and latte art; they’ll find one of Port Augusta's biggest benefactors helping in community spaces.

> "It seemed almost impossible that after fifteen years in technology I could turn back the clock in my own neighbourhood. That notion is exactly what drew me," said Cole.

*An added bonus: Port Augusta’s social services record higher uptake rates in coffeehouse areas post opening. *
