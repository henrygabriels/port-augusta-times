# City Council Unveils New Development at Port Swale

**Port Augusta, November 9, 2025** – In a press release this week, Mayor Susan Blake opened plans to transform  
Port Swale from a historic working harbor into a mixed‑use gateway, fusing residential luxury with recreational shipping. The city’s Vision 2040 blueprint has identified “creative adaptation on soft terrain” in this maritime neighbourhood  

---  

**Historic Roots Meeting Modern Growth**

Port Swale, so far known for tug‑boats servicing north‑regional freight routes, receives the official endorsement for mixed development on the 82‑acre stretch flagged under Town/8 Opinion. While some long‑time yacht harbours residents are wary about heavier commercial influx, “growth and adaptation with respect for the past keep the area vibrant’’ Mayor Blake explained during council meetings Saturday evening.  

---  

**What Residents Can Expect**  

* Residential “low‑rise water‑oriented loft community” (12 plots, ~450 applied acres).
* Parkland extension that promotes safe paths to swale and lagoon in an added 41 ha shoreline conservation board at Riverbend Reserve (link–park-map).
* Heritage‑stressing shipping routes re‑furnished by steel cables (a "classic design meets coastal eco‑modern") replacing the port barge docking for five centuries.

 ---      

**City Economic and Social Predictions**

Pre-construction demand for workforce grew fast from a previous 20% under municipal labour growth targets. With an estimated budget of $73 mln forecast for Phase 1, The Port Augusta Planning Department quoted: "While this phase delivers essential job placements, the public transportation backbone is being retro-rated prior to expansion to maintain commute time below 11 minute," noted infrastructure engineer Carla Valdez.

The development plan comes with an integrated civic kitchen opening plan "Community kitchen concept 2027: >0 community‑based cuisine programs featuring low‑permanent feed in kitchen’s lower hull kitchen."
